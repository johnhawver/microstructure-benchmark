MNQ Microstructure Benchmark — Results¶

End-to-end walk-forward comparison of XGBoost vs LSTM on MNQ 100 ms microstructure bars.

Executive summary¶

  • Data & labels: 15 trading days of Databento MBP-1 (MNQ continuous), triple-barrier labels with ~30/30/40 down/neutral/up split; 12 L1 microstructure features (OFI, microprice tilt, vol, trade imbalance).
  • Predictive performance: XGBoost and LSTM are tied under embargoed walk-forward validation — mean accuracy ~41%, AUC(up vs down) ~0.51; neither beats chance meaningfully on direction.
  • Leakage audit: Random-label control and causality tests pass; real models beat shuffled-label XGB by ~5 pp, confirming features carry weak but genuine signal.
  • Trading simulation: Both models lose money after half-spread + $0.35 commission + 1-bar fill delay; XGB overtrades at low thresholds (Sharpe ≈ −8 to −21), LSTM is quieter but still negative.
  • Latency: Feature pipeline p99 ~4 ms and inference p99 <6 ms — all stages fit inside a 100 ms bar; compute is not the deployment blocker, alpha is.

Run scripts/build_results_figures.py to regenerate all figures below.
